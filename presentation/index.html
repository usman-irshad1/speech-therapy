01 / Motivation & Clinical Need

Addressing the Clinical Gap in Urdu Speech Therapy

Current Clinical Bottlenecks
  • Zero Public Urdu Datasets: No annotated public speech sound disorder datasets or automated tools exist for Urdu-speaking children.
  • Manual & Time-Intensive SLP Scoring: Speech-Language Pathologists must manually listen and annotate phoneme errors, creating long waitlists.
  • Phonetic Complexity of Urdu: 38-44 phonemes including contrastive aspirated plosives (کھ, گھ) and retroflex flaps (ڑ) missed by standard ASR.
  • Subjective Assessment: Absence of objective, quantitative pronunciation metrics (GOP & PCC) for progress tracking.
The AI Pipeline Solution
  • Phoneme-First Two-Stage Architecture: Decouples acoustic representation from disorder classification for maximum clinical interpretability.
  • Self-Supervised Transfer Learning: Leverages cross-lingual representations from IndicWav2Vec-Hindi to conquer low-resource child data constraints.
  • Logit-Based GOP & Alignment: Computes per-phone logit margins to pinpoint exact substitutions, omissions, and distortions.
  • SLP Collaboration & Data Collection: Interactive web-app collector deployed for standardized 35-word elicitation recordings.

04 / Phonetics & G2P Engineering

Canonical Urdu Grapheme-to-Phoneme (G2P) Engine

Phoneme Inventory Coverage (~44 Tokens)
  • Aspirated Plosives: Full support for کھ (/kʰ/), گھ (/gʰ/), بھ (/bʰ/), پھ (/pʰ/), تھ (/tʰ/), دھ (/dʰ/).
  • Retroflex Contrast: Distinct tokens for retroflex stop ٹ (/ʈ/) and retroflex flap ڑ (/ɽ/).
  • Nasalized Vowels: Automatic detection of word-final & medial Noon Ghunna ں (/ɑ̃/, /ẽ/, /ĩ/).
  • Loan Fricatives/Uvulars: Preserves Arabic/Persian loan sounds خ (/x/), غ (/ɣ/), ق (/q/).
G2P Rule Engine Example
from g2p.urdug2p import UrduG2P g2p = UrduG2P() text = "کھڑا" # Standing phonemes = g2p.convert(text) # Output: ['kʰ', 'ə', 'ɽ', 'ɑ'] # Captures aspirated velar + retroflex flap!
The G2P engine forms the dictionary ground-truth for CTC forced alignment and GOP margin evaluation.
05 / Error Detection Engine

Logit-Based Goodness-of-Pronunciation (GOP)

1. Forced Alignment

Performs Viterbi decoding over acoustic logits constrained to target G2P phoneme sequence, outputting exact frame start/end boundaries for every phone.

2. GOP_Margin Scoring

Calculates logit margin:
GOP_Margin = Logit(Target) - Max(Logit(Competitors))
Negative margins indicate competing substitution sounds.

3. Clinical Classification

Flags per-phone speech errors into 4 distinct categories: Substitution Omission Distortion

06 / Engineering Rigor & Diagnostics

Solved Edge Cases & Robustness Fixes

CTC Length Mismatch
BUG: eval_loss = inf

Clips shorter than CTC frame reduction ratio poisoned loss.
Fix: Implemented filter_ctc_length() preprocessing & ctc_zero_infinity=True.

Geminate Phone Alignment
BUG: Frame Collapsing

Grouping by phone value merged adjacent identical sounds (بلی, کتا).
Fix: Switched forced-alignment frame grouping to exact CTC lattice position.

Dynamic Resumption
BUG: GPU Reset Loss

Hardcoded checkpoint paths caused training reset.
Fix: Built dynamic checkpoint detection (resume_from_checkpoint=True) across all trainers.

3-Takes Repetition Protocol

Each child repeats every word 3 separate times during picture-naming tasks.

  • Diagnostic Marker for CAS: Inconsistent error patterns across repeated takes indicate Childhood Apraxia of Speech.
  • Phonological Disorder Marker: Consistent substitution patterns across takes indicate Phonological Disorder.
  • Target Dataset: 35 words × 3 takes = 105 clips/child. Target: ~5,000 total audio clips across clinical & control cohorts.
